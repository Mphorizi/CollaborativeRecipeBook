# Creamy Chicken Pasta Recipe

## Description
A rich and creamy chicken pasta recipe that is quick to prepare and perfect for lunch or dinner.

---

## Ingredients
- 250g pasta
- 2 chicken breasts (sliced)
- 1 cup fresh cream
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1 teaspoon mixed herbs

---

## Instructions

1. Boil the pasta in salted water for 10–12 minutes until tender.
2. Drain the pasta and set aside.
3. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
4. Add the sliced chicken and cook until golden brown.
5. Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute.
6. Pour in the fresh cream and stir well.
7. Add parmesan cheese, salt, black pepper, and mixed herbs.
8. Stir until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
9. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and mix thoroughly.
10. Serve hot.

---

## Preparation Time
- Prep Time: 10 minutes
- Cooking Time: 20 minutes
- Total Time: 30 minutes

---

## Serving Size
Serves 2–3 people
